Request Scribe Assistance

Submit your student and examination details. For ordinary school or college examinations, the system matches writers below the student's current education level. For competitive or government examinations, the selected required qualification is used.

Eligibility rule: For ordinary school/college exams, the writer must be below the student's current education level. All lower levels can be considered; the system is not limited to exactly one academic step.

Examination Papers

Add every paper separately. Examination city is required for every paper because YFII uses the student and volunteer cities to calculate local or intercity travel support. Travel type is decided by YFII; the student does not select it.
