About Us

Who we are

Youth for Inclusive India (YFII) is a community-focused initiative working for inclusive education, youth empowerment, equality, dignity and meaningful social action.

We believe helping someone should not be an act of pity. It should be a shared responsibility carried out with respect, equal opportunity and dignity. Through education support, scribe assistance, resources, youth participation and community initiatives, YFII works to make inclusion practical and accessible.

Our Objectives

Our five objectives are the foundation of our work: Inclusive Education; Scribe & Academic Support; Youth Empowerment; Equality, Dignity & Inclusion; and Social Responsibility & Community Action.
